# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Dinglo Physics Solver aims to be a welcoming educational project. Contributors,
maintainers and users must be treated with respect regardless of experience,
background, identity, location or level of technical knowledge.

## Expected behaviour

- Give constructive, specific and patient feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect different levels of experience.
- Credit other people's work.
- Accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Keep technical discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption or repeated bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Plagiarism or misrepresenting authorship.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional learning space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests
that violate these standards. Serious or repeated violations may result in a
temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub
profile. Reports will be reviewed respectfully and as confidentially as
possible. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces and whenever a participant is publicly
representing the project.
